## Program Name

SpaceX IPO Notification Bot

## Program Description

The SpaceX IPO Notification Bot is a personal SMS alert service that monitors publicly available online news sources (Google News, SEC EDGAR, Reddit) for information about SpaceX going public (IPO). When relevant news is detected, the program sends SMS notifications to the subscriber's registered phone number with article details, source links, and any rumored IPO dates or ticker symbols.

## Message and Data Rates

Standard message and data rates may apply. SMS messages are sent via your mobile carrier, and any charges for receiving text messages as defined by your wireless plan will apply. The program owner is not responsible for any carrier fees associated with receiving SMS messages.

## Message Frequency

- **Confirmed IPO alerts:** Sent immediately upon detection (no limit — these are rare, high-priority events)
- **Rumor/news alerts:** Up to 3 messages per hour maximum
- **Estimated average:** 1–5 messages per day depending on news activity
- **Checking frequency:** Sources are monitored every 5 minutes

## Opt-Out Instructions

You can opt out of receiving messages at any time:

- Text **STOP** to the sending number to immediately stop all SMS notifications from this service
- Text **HELP** to the sending number to receive support information and instructions

After texting **STOP**, you will receive one final confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. To re-subscribe, you must reconfigure the service manually.
